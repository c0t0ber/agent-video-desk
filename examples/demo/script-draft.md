# Weekend ride to the lighthouse

## Hook

I had one free Saturday and a rule: no plan longer than one sentence. Ride to the lighthouse, eat something, come back before dark.

## The route

I drew the line on the map the night before. Forty kilometres along the coast, mostly gravel, one big climb in the middle that I pretended not to see.

## What went wrong

The climb was worse than it looked. Halfway up I was pushing the bike and arguing with myself about turning back.

## The lighthouse

At the top the wind was so strong I could barely stand. But the view was worth every metre of pushing.

## Outro

Next time I will plan two sentences. Maybe three.
